---
name: NOrçamento material hierarchy
description: Product rule for how marketing materials use photos and color.
---

The horizontal material is a conventional professional contact card and must not display photos from work orders. Photos selected or uploaded for marketing materials belong only in the vertical presentation.

The vertical presentation should introduce the gallery with an “Últimos serviços” heading. Each photo belongs in a fixed-size card, with the optional user-written description rendered below the image rather than floating over it.

The materials should feel institutional and trustworthy rather than playful: use restrained graphite, sand, and muted terracotta accents instead of saturated orange or decorative shapes.

**Why:** the card and the presentation serve different sharing contexts; mixing work-order imagery into the compact card made the primary identity piece feel informal and visually loud.

**How to apply:** preserve the photo-free horizontal preview and PNG export, keep photos in the vertical preview/export, and keep future styling changes subdued across web and mobile.
